This article by Arturo Sánchez Jiménez originally appeared in the May 16, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left-wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City — China reaffirmed its "firm support" for Mexico in maintaining its independence and autonomy, and expressed its willingness to deepen the Comprehensive Strategic Partnership between both countries on the basis of mutual respect and reciprocal benefit, the Chinese embassy in Mexico reported on Saturday.

The position was expressed by the Chinese ambassador to Mexico, Chen Daojiang, in a meeting held on Friday with the new foreign minister, Roberto Velasco Álvarez, which was also attended by the undersecretary, María Teresa Mercado Pérez.

According to the Chinese diplomatic mission, Chen noted that relations between both countries "have deepened" under the leadership of President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo and President Xi Jinping, and highlighted that the potential for bilateral cooperation continues to expand.

The ambassador also recalled that on May 13, the first anniversary was marked of the announcement made by Chinese President Xi Jinping regarding the five programs for building a community with a shared future between China and Latin America and the Caribbean.

He stated that over the past year, cooperation between China and the region has registered "positive progress" in various areas, and that Beijing seeks to work jointly with Mexico to promote the implementation of those programs on Mexican soil "for the benefit of the peoples of both countries."

For his part, Velasco, according to the Chinese embassy, affirmed that the friendship between both nations has "deep historical roots" and underscored that the Mexican government attaches great importance to the development of the bilateral relationship.

According to the Chinese embassy, the foreign minister also reiterated that Mexico maintains its adherence to the "One China" principle and expressed the willingness to strengthen high-level exchanges and deepen practical cooperation between both nations to achieve "even more fruitful" results in the bilateral relationship.
