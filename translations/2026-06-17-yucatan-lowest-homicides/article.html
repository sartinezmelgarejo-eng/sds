This article by Luis A. Boffil Gómez originally appeared in the June 17, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mérida, Yuc. — The state of Yucatán registered only 12 intentional homicide victims between January and May 2026, placing the state with the lowest incidence of this crime among the 32 states of the country in that same period, reported figures from the Executive Secretariat of the National Public Security System (Sesnsp).

In this way, the national average of intentional homicide victims during the period from January to May 2026 was 50.4 cases daily, the lowest figure for a first four-month period and fifth month since 2016. From these figures, 6 percent is derived with respect to Yucatán and its 12 intentional homicide victims from January to May of this year. At the monthly level, May 2026 closed with a national average of 47.3 victims per day, which represented a historic decrease compared to the start of the administration in September 2024, according to Sesnsp data.

According to the federal agency, this result is part of the ongoing work driven by the State Government through coordination among security, justice administration, and crime prevention institutions, as well as surveillance, intelligence, and citizen-service actions throughout the territory.

The data published by Sesnsp confirm that Yucatán –under the Government of Morena member Joaquín Díaz Mena– maintains favorable indicators in public security, which contributes to preserving conditions of peace, stability, and trust for Yucatecan families.

Likewise, the Peace Index Mexico 2026 report confirmed Yucatán's leadership as the most peaceful state in the country, maintaining the first national position with a rating of 1.279 points.

In this regard, Yucatán has consistently registered the lowest homicide rate in the country. In 2025, its rate of 1.8 deaths per 100,000 inhabitants was ten times lower than the national rate of 18.1 per hundred thousand inhabitants. Mérida, the capital of the State, had the lowest homicide rate of any state capital, with around 1.6 homicides per hundred thousand inhabitants, according to this year's 2026 Peace Index report.

And in 2025, according to this year's 2026 Peace Index report, the two states with the lowest organized-crime rates, by far, were Yucatán and Tlaxcala, whose rates were respectively 11 and six times lower than the national rate.

Likewise, Yucatán had the lowest rate of firearm-related crimes in Mexico. With only 0.3 incidents per 100,000 inhabitants, its rate was more than 60 times lower than the national rate and more than four times lower than the rate of the second-best-ranked state, Coahuila. This was driven by the low number of homicides committed with firearms in Yucatán. The state had the lowest overall homicide rate in Mexico last year, and only 4.7% of these homicides were committed with firearms, the smallest proportion in the country.

On the other hand, the Peace Index also evaluated the economic impact of violence in per capita terms. In 2025, the per capita cost ranged between 10,785 pesos in Yucatán and 70,123 pesos in Colima, meaning that the cost of violence in the least peaceful state of the country was almost seven times greater than in the most peaceful.

Security continues to be a priority for the State Government, so coordinated actions are maintained with federal, state, and municipal authorities to prevent crimes, attend to citizen reports, and strengthen the institutional presence in neighborhoods, communities, and municipalities.
