This article by Darylh Rodríguez originally appeared in the June 29, 2026 edition of Revista Contralínea, a Mexican investigative magazine.

The former general director of Petróleos Mexicanos (Pemex), Víctor Rodríguez Padilla, will face "the full weight of the law" for the assaults committed against his wife, the academic María Felicia Jiménez, said President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo, noting that the case will not go unpunished because there will be no cronyism nor any protection that is not in favor of the victim. "The law is the same for everyone, not one way for some and another way for others."

The president's statement came after images circulated this weekend of the former federal official assaulting the victim, even in front of one of their young children, at a residence located in the municipality of Emiliano Zapata, near Cuernavaca. In them, he is seen crudely subduing his wife with blows, shoves, and hair-pulling, actions that constitute clear domestic violence.

In response to these events, the Morelos Attorney General's Office announced that it has already opened an investigation file against the former director of the state-owned company for his alleged involvement in the assault. In this context, the head of the Federal Executive —who has held up the banner of feminism as one of the main hallmarks of her term— said that it will be the autonomous institution that determines the criminal penalty.

"It was the Ministry of Women that got in contact with the victim, and let the law be applied. The full weight of the law. We are not going to protect anyone in the face of an act like this. So, the Morelos Attorney General's Office has to step in because it happened in the state of Morelos. So, it is a state crime and the law has to be applied because there can be no violence against women," President Sheinbaum maintained at her morning press conference.

With this, Sheinbaum clarified that Rodríguez Padilla will also not hold any other position within the federal public administration, since, as will be recalled, the former official was in the process of joining the National Institute of Electricity and Clean Energies (INEEL) of the Federal Electricity Commission (CFE), after his departure from Pemex on May 14, 2026. However, President Sheinbaum explained that said appointment had not yet been formalized and, therefore, was definitively canceled.

In this way, the president made clear that the former Pemex director will not be considered for any other public post in the near future. "No, no, not anymore," she responded bluntly when asked about the possibility that he could take on another position in public service, and she stressed that in her government there will be no tolerance for cases of violence against women, regardless of the hierarchical level or political closeness of those involved.

The images of the case that were circulated have provoked strong indignation at the national level, by explicitly displaying the physical aggression carried out by a senior former federal official against his wife. Regarding them in particular, the victim —who also holds a doctorate in Nuclear Physics Engineering— decided to break the silence and make her complaint public through social media.

The post was accompanied by a message in which María Felicia Jiménez recounted the context of violence that she says she experienced at the hands of her still-husband. With it, she requested the intervention of President Claudia Sheinbaum, out of fear of reprisals stemming from the political influence of the former director of Petróleos Mexicanos.

"At home, a high-ranking official of the current government beats his wife. Breaking the silence meant being left without a job, without money, without anywhere to live, and having my children taken from me simply because of his closeness to the upper echelons of the Presidency," the academic denounced, making visible not only the physical violence but also the economic, psychological, and power-based violence that, according to her testimony, had for years made it difficult to report.
