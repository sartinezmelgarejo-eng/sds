This article by La Redacción originally appeared in the June 30, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. President Claudia Sheinbaum will watch a match of the national team for the second time so far in the Soccer World Cup at a Fan Fest, together with the people who come to these sites with giant screens.

Through a message on social media, the President reported: "From Azcapotzalco: Go Mexico!"

Accompanied by the Head of Government of Mexico City, the President went to Parque Tezozómoc to watch the round-of-32 match between Mexico and Ecuador, which was delayed one hour by the electrical storm that broke out around the Estadio Ciudad de México.
