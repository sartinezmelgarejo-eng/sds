This article by Gustavo Castillo García originally appeared in the July 7, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. Officers of the Investigation Police of the Attorney General's Office of Mexico City (FGJCDMX) arrested the former director of Petróleos Mexicanos (Pemex), Víctor Rodríguez Padilla, in the Benito Juárez borough, after he was reported by his wife, María Felicia Jiménez Lavie, for domestic violence.

The former official was filmed beating his wife, which generated various reactions of condemnation over the images in which he strikes his partner, while their children witnessed what they experienced in their home.

Rodríguez Padilla was reported at the end of June 2026 in Mexico City for domestic violence, and his arrest was carried out on the basis of a ministerial summons order, according to federal sources.

The National Registry of Detentions of the Ministry of Security and Citizen Protection (SSPC) documented that the place of arrest was on Monte Albán street at its crossing with Diagonal San Antonio, in the Narvarte neighborhood.

The arrest was carried out at 5:32 p.m. by officers of the Investigation Police, and he was placed at the disposal of the capital's Public Prosecutor's Office, since the complaint against him was filed with the Prosecutor's Office for the Investigation of the Crime of Domestic Violence.
