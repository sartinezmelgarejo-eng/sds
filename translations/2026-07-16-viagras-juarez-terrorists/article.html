This article by La Jornada's editorial staff originally appeared in the July 16, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. The United States State Department on Thursday designated the Juárez Cartel and Los Viagras as Foreign Terrorist Organizations (FTO) and Specially Designated Global Terrorists (SDGT), a day after the Treasury Department also classified them as "foreign and global terrorist organizations."

In the statement, it accuses the groups of being "violent narco-terrorist cartels" and of having committed numerous attacks against Americans, Mexican security forces, and civilians, including the 2019 massacre in Sonora in which nine U.S. citizens were killed.

The department affirmed that President Donald Trump's administration will continue to use all the resources at its disposal to "protect" the country, by keeping the streets free of poison and disrupting the narco-terrorists' sources of financing.

Designations as terrorist organizations expose and isolate entities and individuals, denying them access to the U.S. financial system and to the resources needed to carry out attacks.

Likewise, it blocks all the assets and property interests of the designated persons that are in the United States or that are in the possession or under the control of a U.S. citizen.
