This article by La Redacción originally appeared in the August 2, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

United States President Donald Trump shared on his social network this Sunday an article by conservative host and commentator Bill O'Reilly, published this past March, in which he argues that "Mexico is not a friend of the United States" and levels criticism against President Claudia Sheinbaum over her strategy toward the drug-trafficking cartels.

In the text, originally circulated on March 11 and reposted on Truth Social by Trump without adding any comment, O'Reilly asserts that the Mexican government is not a friend of the United States and that it "has been corrupt for decades," without evidence, accusing President Sheinbaum of preventing U.S. authorities from operating on Mexican territory to combat the criminal groups.

He also dismisses the President's statements about the responsibility of drug consumption in the United States and the illegal trafficking of weapons into Mexico.

The commentator further maintains that the cartels "have murdered more than a million people since the 1990s," without presenting evidence or sources to support that claim. Nor does he provide proof to back other accusations, such as the alleged refusal of the Mexican government to allow the participation of U.S. authorities in operations against organized crime.
