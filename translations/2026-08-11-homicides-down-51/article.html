This article by Alonso Urrutia and Laura Poy originally appeared in the August 11, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left-wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. As a result of the National Security strategy, President Claudia Sheinbaum announced that between September 2024—the last month of the previous administration—and July 2026, the reduction in homicides has already reached 51 percent. That is, over that period daily killings went from 86.9 to 42.5 this past July, that is, 44 fewer homicides per day.

Sheinbaum emphasized that halving the country's homicides is due to the comprehensive functioning of the strategy, noting that during this administration sixty thousand criminals have been arrested.

Referring to the increase in arrests, Sheinbaum acknowledged that this has had repercussions on the prison population, particularly in state prisons. For this reason, she said that work is already underway on the possibility of expanding six state prisons under a new humanist approach, which has nothing to do with the schemes developed in other countries.

In breaking down the evolution of this crime, the head of the Executive Secretariat of the National Public Security System, Marcela Figueroa, asserted that seven states account for 49 percent of the total violent deaths recorded in the country.

She highlighted that Guanajuato remains the most violent state, stressing that homicides in the state went from 12.7 per day in February 2025 to 3.1 this past July, that is, a 76 percent reduction.

She mentioned that Guanajuato accounts for 8.6 percent of the country's total killings, followed by Baja California, which represents 8.2 percent, and Chihuahua with 8.1 percent. In the case of Baja California, the incidence of homicides fell 48 percent—below the national average—going from 7.1 homicides per day to 3.7.

She said that these projects have not yet been precisely determined but are being analyzed so they can begin soon to avoid overcrowding, that is, under a humanist scheme.

For the president, the consolidation of the National Guard has been fundamental in confronting crime, in addition to the strengthened use of intelligence, noting that today 220 new intelligence investigators are graduating to reinforce the use of intelligence in the security strategy.
