Mexico City, August 30 (SinEmbargo).— The national president of Morena, Ariadna Montiel, delivered a forceful message to those aspiring to run for a candidacy representing the party, stating that, should they win the internal poll, if it comes to light that they have "a questionable past," that candidacy will not be endorsed.

During the fifth plenary meeting of Morena's parliamentary group in the Senate, Montiel noted that the people elected a legislative representation made up mostly of her movement, together with the President, and that this implies a "great historic responsibility."

"What we will not allow is for people with unmentionable ties to represent us," she affirmed. She reiterated the commitment to never accept "someone who has pending accounts, a questionable past, or actions that prevent them from being part of this transformation."
