This article by Andrea Becerril and Georgina Saldierna originally appeared in the August 31, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. Almost unanimously, the Senate of the Republic elected Morena member Higinio Martínez Miranda as president of the Governing Board of that chamber for the coming legislative year, which begins this Tuesday. The final vote was 116 of the 117 senators present in favor of the slate headed by the legislator from the State of Mexico, who, after being sworn in, pledged to conduct the work of this body of Congress with "impartiality, objectivity, and absolute respect for the voice" of its 128 members.

Martínez Miranda maintained that absolute respect for the freedom of expression of all legislators would be guaranteed and that no one would be reprimanded for what they say. He noted that they could use the floor as they wish, although each one would be responsible for what they express at the podium. He only asked them for "a minimum of consideration" and to respect the law and the rules.

He recalled, however, a phrase by the writer José Emilio Pacheco to the effect that "there are those who confuse insult and slander as mere expressions of freedom of expression."

Eleven Senators Missed the Session

Eleven senators from different parliamentary groups were absent from the Senate's installation session, in which Martínez Miranda was elected, among them Gerardo Fernández Noroña. On the Governing Board, Morena member Verónica Camino and Jorge Carlos Ramírez Marín, of the PVEM, repeated as vice presidents.

Martínez Miranda was accompanied by his family and several mayors from the State of Mexico, who joined the expressions of support from his caucus. During his remarks, he also paid tribute to Heberto Castillo, with whom, he recalled, he got his start in politics.

Laura Itzel Castillo's Tenure Recognized

Previously, his predecessor, Laura Itzel Castillo, received strong applause as she went up to the Governing Board in a wheelchair. The legislator said that during her tenure the chamber managed to strengthen debate and respect among all its members, which was recognized by her Morena colleagues, as well as by senators from the PAN, PRI, and Movimiento Ciudadano.

Enrique Inzunza Returns to His Seat

Senator Enrique Inzunza Cázarez also attended the session; despite having left Morena's parliamentary group, he sat in the same seat he has occupied since September 1, 2024. There was anticipation about the reception the Sinaloa legislator would get; however, no incidents occurred.

Several of his former caucus colleagues greeted him affectionately, among them Alfonso Cepeda, Lucía Trasviña, and Óscar Cantón Zetina, as well as PAN member Susana Zatarain, his seatmate.

When Inzunza Cázarez went up to cast his vote in the ballot box, a brief murmur was heard from the section occupied by PAN and PRI legislators, without the situation escalating. Afterward, the senator posed for photographs.
