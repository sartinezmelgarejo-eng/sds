This article by La Jornada's editorial staff (La Redacción) originally appeared in the September 2, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left-wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. Former president of Colombia Gustavo Petro visited Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo this Wednesday. During his visit to President Sheinbaum, Petro warned about the risks of interference from the United States in Latin America's electoral processes.

The Colombian—who is on a visit to our country—maintained that Mexico must watch what happens in the region's electoral processes, such as in Brazil, as well as the advance of right-wing forces, ahead of Mexico's 2027 midterm elections and the 2030 presidential election.

"I have just come from speaking with the president of the United Mexican States, @Claudiashein," he posted on his X account, alongside a photograph from the Zócalo of Mexico City.

After meeting with the Mexican president, Petro said that they spoke about what he considered "a new political reality in the region," linked to operations originating outside the countries where the elections take place.

"The pressing issue is the artificial new political reality. We could call it that based on all these investigations that have been carried out from Bolivia and that cover all of the Americas, because its real center is in Washington and its financing is in Israel," he said.

The former president of Colombia has been on a visit to Mexico since last Monday and on Wednesday will give a keynote lecture at the UNAM.

On leaving the National Palace, he commented that Sheinbaum Pardo invited him to stay in Mexico to carry out academic activities.

"She invited me to some intellectual, academic coordination work that I don't dislike doing, but I decided to live in Colombia."
