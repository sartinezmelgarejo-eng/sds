This article by Arturo Sánchez Jiménez originally appeared in the September 2, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left-wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. Secretary of Foreign Affairs Roberto Velasco Álvarez will make a working visit to the People's Republic of China on September 7, the Secretariat of Foreign Affairs (SRE) reported this Wednesday through its account on the social network X. During his stay, the foreign minister will hold a meeting with his Chinese counterpart, Foreign Minister Wang Yi.

As part of his agenda, Velasco will also inaugurate the Consulate of Mexico in the city of Chongqing. According to the agency, the visit "will strengthen political dialogue and the cooperation agenda between the two countries."

The trip adds to previous meetings between representatives of the two foreign ministries. In May 2025, in the framework of the IV Ministerial Meeting of the CELAC-China Forum, then-Foreign Minister Juan Ramón de la Fuente met with Wang Yi, an occasion on which both officials highlighted the relationship of respect between Mexico and China and their shared interest in strengthening bilateral cooperation.

This will be Velasco's second trip to Asia in recent weeks, as he visited South Korea from August 23 to 25.
