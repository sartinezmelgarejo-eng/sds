This article by La Redacción originally appeared in the September 5, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left-wing daily newspaper.

Colima, Colima.— To reinforce her claim that "we are never going to finish paying" the debt from the 1990s bank bailout engineered by Ernesto Zedillo, President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo reported that every two years "we pay 50 billion pesos from the public budget to the IPAB (Institute for the Protection of Bank Savings, successor to the Bank Fund for the Protection of Savings, Fobaproa)" in bonds.

"But that only corresponds to a part of the interest on that debt. For the principal and the other part of the interest, debt has to be taken on to pay the IPAB," according to the data sent to her by the Finance Secretary, Edgar Amador Zamora. You see —she commented— that "when you take on a debt, you pay off principal and interest to gradually reduce it." In the case of Fobaproa, "only a part of the interest is paid, with bonds," she maintained.

"That is why I maintain that, incredibly, the Fobaproa will never be paid off; that was the irresponsibility of those governments of the past," noting that Zedillo was the successor of Carlos Salinas de Gortari (1988-1994), at the end of whose term "a tremendous economic crisis came. The peso was devalued, interest rates rose, and so there were no resources in the country."

She commented that, already in his own administration, Zedillo "decided to rescue the bankers and the big businessmen with the people's money, and the person who, for example, had bought a tractor, and whose debt suddenly shot up to the sky so that they couldn't pay — he did not help them."

"They had to hand over the tractor and sometimes even their house and everything they had. It was a time when many people from different strata of the country had to migrate to the United States because there were no options here."

"That is why I ask you: does the people of Mexico want to return to the past? We are going to continue in this government, defending the transformation. That is the path."
