This article by Julio Gutiérrez originally appeared in the September 7, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. The federal government plans to maintain a national output of liquid hydrocarbons of 1.8 million barrels per day through 2039, as part of the strategy to strengthen domestic supply and reduce dependence on fuel imports. This is part of the strategy laid out in the Hydrocarbons Sector Development Plan, published this Monday by the Ministry of Energy (Sener) in the Official Gazette of the Federation (DOF). To reach that goal, the administration envisions the participation of Petróleos Mexicanos (Pemex), private companies, and mixed-development projects. The scenario incorporates exploration and extraction projects, current contracts, and new developments, in addition to secondary and enhanced recovery processes.

For natural gas, Sener expects to maintain production close to 5 billion cubic feet per day through 2039. The document anticipates that production will surpass that level in 2035, with 5.362 billion cubic feet per day, and reach a maximum of 6.658 billion in 2038. Increasing national gas production is one of the main objectives of the plan, given the growing dependence on imports. It specifies that, in 2024, 89 percent of natural gas purchases abroad were made by private companies.

Refining Objectives

On refining, the government expects the facilities of the National Refining System to operate at an average of 80 percent of their capacity, with the participation of the Deer Park refinery in the United States. The goal is to reach a processing capacity of up to 1.695 million barrels per day and raise the production of gasoline, diesel, and jet fuel, while reducing the production of fuel oil.

It also envisions the recovery of the petrochemical industry through the rehabilitation of the Morelos and La Cangrejera complexes, which is expected to raise the production of ethylene derivatives from 176,000 tons per year in 2025 to 1,029,000 tons starting in 2030. For aromatics, production would go from 78,000 tons to 329,000 tons per year starting in 2027. The strategy also includes raising urea production to reduce dependence on fertilizer imports.

Increasing Reserves

The Plan presented by Sener establishes a 15-year horizon for hydrocarbon policy and proposes that national production be directed primarily to the domestic market, with a gradual reduction of crude oil and energy product exports as their use within the country increases.

And as part of the exploration strategy, the federal government plans to add approximately 7.292 billion barrels of crude oil equivalent in 3P reserves between 2026 and 2039, with the goal of halting the decline in national hydrocarbon reserves. The document notes that for the first five years of the period it plans to add 3.212 billion barrels, while exploration activities will initially concentrate in the oil provinces of the Southeastern Basins and Veracruz.
