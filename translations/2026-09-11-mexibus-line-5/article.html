This article by Silvia Chávez González originally appeared in the September 11, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left-wing daily newspaper.

Tlalnepantla, State of Mexico. Construction of the Lechería–El Rosario Line 5 of the Mexibús is registering 16 percent progress, with preliminary foundation work and induced works, in addition to the layout and leveling of the space intended for the confined lane along Doctor Gustavo Baz Avenue; the removal of "tree species" during the works will be compensated.

Juan Hugo de la Rosa García, Secretary of Mobility, reported that, to speed up the project, the State of Mexico Mass Transit and Cable Car System (SITRAMYTEM) established five work fronts—four in the State of Mexico and one in Mexico City—and work is currently under way at 12 of the 30 stations that will make up this public transit system in the western Valley of Mexico.

Line 5 of the Mexibús will benefit more than 137,000 residents of Naucalpan, Tlalnepantla, Cuautitlán Izcalli, and Tultitlán, as well as residents of the Azcapotzalco borough in Mexico City. It will run 30 kilometers, mainly along Vía Gustavo Baz. The service will have 58 latest-generation units and will connect with the Lechería station of the Suburban Train, Line 2 of the Mexibús, and the El Rosario Modal Transfer Center in Mexico City.

The official stressed that the works comply with the applicable environmental measures and provisions. In addition, the corresponding arrangements were made with environmental and municipal authorities for the management of the solid waste generated by the construction.

Regarding the trees along the line's route, he stressed that "the tree species that had to be removed have physical, economic, or equivalent compensation measures."

Likewise, during the works, conservation and correction is being carried out on lines carrying potable and treated water, drainage, sewage, telecommunications, and natural gas, in order to prevent impacts on the public and to guarantee the efficient operation of the new transit system. With the construction of Line 5 of the Mexibús, the State of Mexico government will address the mobility needs of the Valley of Mexico's population and expand the options for safe, comfortable, agile, and efficient public transportation.
