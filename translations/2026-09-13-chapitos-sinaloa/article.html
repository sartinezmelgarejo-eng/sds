This article by José Shaddai Olvera Torres originally appeared in the September 13, 2026 edition of El Chamuco.

The operation took place in Escuinapa, where authorities seized 12 long guns, magazines, ammunition, and tactical equipment.

Members of the III Military Region and the 9th Military Zone reported the detention of 11 alleged members of Los Chapitos, a faction of the Sinaloa Cartel, during an operation in the municipality of Escuinapa, Sinaloa.

The detention was carried out by personnel of the Mexican Army, in coordination with the Special Operations Group of the Secretariat of Public Security of Sinaloa.

The military and state personnel were conducting ground reconnaissance and surveillance patrols in the town of La Isla del Bosque when they carried out the operation.

Weapons and Tactical Equipment Seized

According to the information provided by the authorities, the 11 alleged members of Los Chapitos were detained in the act.

During the operation, 12 long guns, magazines, ammunition of various calibers, and tactical equipment were seized.

The detainees were placed at the disposal of the corresponding authorities to determine their legal status.

Defense Links Detainees to "Casco"

According to information from the Secretariat of National Defense (Defensa), the detainees would be under the command of Óscar Luciano Martínez Larios, alias Casco, identified as a regional boss of Los Chapitos.

The agency also identifies Martínez Larios as an alleged participant in the kidnapping and murder of 10 workers of the mining company Canam/Vizsla Silver, which occurred this past January 23 in the municipality of La Concordia, Sinaloa.

The link between the detainees and Martínez Larios, as well as the accusations against him, correspond to allegations by the authorities and must be determined by the competent judicial bodies.
