This article originally appeared in the September 20, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left-wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. The United States ambassador to Mexico, Ronald Johnson, celebrated on Sunday that during the government of President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo, more than 2,700 narco-labs and methamphetamine production sites have been disabled, actions that, he acknowledged, have reduced the flow of drugs to the communities of both countries.

On social media, Johnson highlighted the role of Mexican authorities in dismantling four clandestine labs this past Saturday in Michoacán and Guerrero, where more than 10 tons of chemical precursors and drug-making equipment were seized.

"Mexican authorities dismantled four clandestine drug labs in Michoacán and Guerrero, where they seized and destroyed more than 10 metric tons of chemical precursors. Even after being shot at by organized crime, they completed the operation. More than 2,700 clandestine labs and methamphetamine production sites have been put out of commission under Mexico's current government. #JuntosLogramosMás," he posted.
