(September 21, 2026).— The president denied having intervened in the process to choose coordinators and maintained that the method seeks to avoid decisions by "dedazo" (top-down handpicking).

President Claudia Sheinbaum defended the polling method used by Morena to select its state coordinators and denied having intervened in the organization or application of the surveys.

During La Mañanera del Pueblo, the president attributed the discontent that arose after the designations to the displeasure of those who had aspired to occupy those positions and were ultimately not selected.

"There will always be people who would have wanted to become coordinator and don't, so there will always be some discontent from the one who didn't make it," she said.

Sheinbaum Rejects Any "Dedazo"

Sheinbaum explained that Morena uses the surveys as a mechanism to prevent the selection of coordinators from depending directly on the party leadership.

According to her explanation, the procedure includes 10 questions with different weights, whose results are combined to obtain a final score.

The president maintained that this mechanism seeks to ensure the decision does not fall to one person or to the party leadership.

In that sense, she rejected that there is any "dedazo" in the internal process. This position aligns with previous statements in which Sheinbaum has held that Morena's candidacies and internal decisions are determined through polls.

Asked about the conduct of the current surveys, Sheinbaum said she did not intervene in their application and that she trusts the team responsible.

The president said that the organization of the process corresponds to the team in charge of the measurements and not to her directly.

The president also maintained that the results of the surveys must be made public.

Publishing the measurements would make it possible to know the results of the coordinator-selection process and contrast them with the perceptions that have circulated in the media and on social networks.

"You look at the media and social networks, and it's not necessarily what people think," she noted.

Morena's decisions are part of the process leading up to the 2027 elections, in which various popularly elected offices will be renewed. The president has previously noted that in Morena's case, candidacies are determined through polls and that the aim is to maintain parity.
