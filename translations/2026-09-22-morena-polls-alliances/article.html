This article by Daniela Barragán and José Manuel Fuentes originally appeared in the September 22, 2026 edition of SinEmbargo.

Mexico City, September 22 (SinEmbargo).— The Coordinator of the National Elections Commission of Morena, Citlalli Hernández, stated that the alliance with the Labor Party (PT) and the Ecologist Green Party of Mexico (PVEM) is the “strongest in the country,” in response to statements by PT member Reginaldo Sandoval warning of a possible rupture over the results of the selection of the Coordinators for the Defense of the Transformation, whose selection polls will now be made public.

“I think it is important to go into an electoral process in which three parties that have walked together, which I maintain is the strongest coalition in the country, with the largest and strongest party in the country, which is Morena, face an election where the PRIAN will surely unite.”

In an interview with journalists Daniela Barragán and José Manuel Fuentes for the program “Café y Noticias,” broadcast on the SinEmbargo Al Aire YouTube channel, Citlalli Hernández said that “good politics is built through dialogue, through agreement on the common good, which in this case with our allies, the common good has been support for our President Claudia Sheinbaum, the continuity of the Transformation project, an electoral strategy that allows us to continue being a very strong coalition.”

The Coordinator of the guinda party’s Elections Commission said she is in constant dialogue with the Labor Party. She stated that if the party backs Reginaldo Sandoval’s statements, then a space for dialogue will be opened to “settle differences,” although she noted that she has no official position from the PT.

Reginaldo Sandoval had also indicated that his party is considering conducting “mirror polls” in the states where there is disagreement. He even said his party is open to the possibility that aspirants dissatisfied with Morena’s decisions could seek to compete under the PT banner. Citlalli Hernández indicated that this is permitted to provide greater certainty, although the agreements were established from the beginning.

“The three parties agreed that we would go to a poll. The poll already known, with Morena’s already known method. There could be mirror polls, of course, to give greater certainty to that sample survey. We said that whoever won, we would respect it and close ranks under the conviction and the will to go as a coalition in 16 states, leaving, let’s say, San Luis Potosí aside. That is the agreement as of today. I have no other official proposal or position from the Labor Party. We reviewed the names before sending them to polls.”

She stated that there was transparency throughout the process and both parties were consulted at every step, so she says she “does not know what strategy the Labor Party has internally, because what is real is that their proposals were indeed polled; the polls we are presenting to the aspirants include both the PT’s person and the Green’s person.”

Morena Will Make Polls Public: Ariadna Montiel

Morena’s national leader, Ariadna Montiel, reported that the party will publish the polls with which the coordinators of the Fourth Transformation were defined in the states that will hold midterm elections in 2027.

During the press conference of Morena’s National Executive Committee (CEN), she explained that the results will be made public once the presentation of all the people designated to lead the coordinations concludes.

“Once we have made the presentations in their entirety, we are going to invite you [the media] so they can tell you about them, and so that we can make them known in detail, one by one, so that you have access to this information,” she said.

In parallel, Montiel recalled that the poll results are presented to all the people participating in the process, including the allied parties, before announcing who wins the coordination.

To define the profiles that took part in the polling, Morena first carried out a telephone exercise among its aspirants, which considered their name recognition. From that filter, four party profiles were selected, who later took part in the polls alongside the aspirants proposed by the Labor Party and the PVEM.

“We do this process with our compañeros; there is no candidate who can say he didn’t see it,” she emphasized.

The PT Warns of a Rupture

The PT’s coordinator in the Chamber of Deputies, Reginaldo Sandoval, warned of a possible rupture in the coalition with Morena heading into the 2027 elections if the guinda party does not address its proposals.

In an interview with the media, Sandoval explained that the PT is keeping its participation in Morena’s process on hold because it does not agree with some decisions, nor does it consider the methodology sufficiently clear. This is in addition to believing that his party can compete and win on its own.

Verbatim, he said: “It can indeed fracture if Morena doesn’t understand. I also understand that that is why it has the attitude that it feels overconfident, feels capable; since there is no adversary on the right, it understands that it can have a floor and so it disdains its allies. It feels it can go alone; perhaps that is what we read, and it is totally wrong in that logic.”

He considered that there is “a lot of arrogance” in Morena and indicated that the party would be proceeding from the idea that it can compete alone due to the absence, so far, of a right-wing political force representing an electoral counterweight.

He stated that an eventual rupture will depend on Morena’s response to his proposals and reproached Ariadna Montiel’s party for assuming it can compete without its allies.
