This article by Alma E. Muñoz originally appeared in the September 25, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Villahermosa, Tabasco. After describing as interesting the rapprochement between the two great nations with the meeting in Washington of the presidents of the United States, Donald Trump, and of China, Xi Jinping, the head of the Executive, Claudia Sheinbaum, said she agrees with the Chinese leader’s view that China’s development does not necessarily imply entering into contradiction with the U.S. stance under the logic of Make America Great Again.

Sheinbaum considered this rapprochement important because, since they are the two great powers in the world, their rivalry generates uncertainty in trade relations across the globe.

On the other hand, Sheinbaum said that the talks between Mexico and the United States toward a trade agreement are advancing, which will involve an increase in Mexican imports from that country; Mexico, on its own, is already the country that buys the most products from the United States.

She also recalled her presence in China next November during the meeting of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum (APEC), particularly because next year Mexico will be the host, and it is customary for the leader of the next host to attend in order to receive the baton.

Although the agenda she will have will be announced in due course, she commented that she is interested in meeting with Xi Jinping.

She emphasized that in the trade talks, Mexico is seeking the reduction of tariffs on steel, aluminum, and the vehicles that are exported. She noted that the negotiations have intensified and there is a determination to reach an agreement. On many issues progress has been made, but on others it has not been possible, particularly because they touch on our sovereignty, and that Mexico cannot accept.
