This article by Arturo Daen originally appeared in the September 25, 2026 edition of SinEmbargo, an independent Mexican digital news outlet.

The Michoacán Prosecutor's Office denied the case of a grave in Michoacán with 1,600 little baby teeth, reported by journalist Óscar Balderas and amplified by Televisión Azteca, Alito Moreno, and Adela Micha, among others.

Mexico City, September 25 (SinEmbargo).– The story of the supposed discovery of a clandestine grave in Zacapu, Michoacán, with "1,600 little baby teeth," spread on social media starting from a column by commentator Óscar Balderas. The opposition threw itself into amplifying the story without documentary evidence and blaming the "4T," however, on Thursday night the Michoacán Prosecutor's Office denied having any record or formal complaint about it.

The PRI, politicians from the PAN and from Somos, TV Azteca, and commentators close to Ricardo Salinas Pliego (including his apparatus of troll accounts and content creators such as Chumel Torres, Arturo Villegas, Índira Kempis, Kike Mireles, Marcel Carrillo, and Jimena Villicaña), as well as La Derecha Diario of Javier Negre and Fernando Cerimedo, spread the case, which had already been reported by a searching mother in 2023, Margarita López, and which resurfaced from Balderas's text.

"Michoacán is the reflection of the horror and the criminal complicity of the 4T," posted Manuel López San Martín, a TV Azteca journalist, while interviewing Balderas on television. "Why do they kill babies? The UNTHINKABLE grave with THOUSANDS of baby teeth," he tweeted. Meanwhile Adela Micha, in addition to presenting a video titled "There Were Baby Teeth. The Worst Criminal Grave in the History of Mexico," in which images of the supposed discovery of remains are interspersed with illustrations made with artificial intelligence.

The PRI leader, Alejandro Moreno Cárdenas, posted on X, adding a screenshot of the TV Azteca report: "The discovery of 1,600 baby teeth in a clandestine grave in Zacapu, Michoacán, is an atrocity that shakes and horrifies. We are talking about dental remains of minors found underground. It is the reflection of the brutal crisis of violence and of the level of criminal barbarity that Mexico is living through."

And Ricardo Salinas Pliego, picking up Óscar Balderas's column, said: "It causes me deep sadness to know that this happens in Mexico and that the #NarcoRégimen covers up for the guilty, allies itself with them to win elections, and defends them under the pretext of protecting national sovereignty."

In Balderas's text, published by the newspaper Milenio, it is stated that an expert from the Office of the Attorney General of the Republic (FGR), "Braulio N.," allegedly corroborated in 2021 that a bone remain located in a grave in Zacapu was a baby tooth (a claim that was not backed by any document or evidence).

Then, according to his column, the collective founded by the searching mother and former local deputy for the Green Ecologist Party, Margarita López, continued its search and so, as of September 22, it had found "some 1,600 little baby teeth, the evidence that hundreds of children from one to six years old were illegally incinerated in a grave in Zacapu."

In an interview with Adela Micha, Balderas mentioned that those bone remains had allegedly ended up in the hands of FGR personnel. But in its statement, the Michoacán Prosecutor's Office denied that version. "There is permanent coordination with the FGR, who also have no record of said discovery," it stated.

Since September 9, the searching mother Margarita López of the collective Buscando Cuerpos en Todo México, whom Balderas took up as a source for his column, has taken part in a sit-in in front of the facilities of the Office of the Attorney General of the Republic. Her protest has had coverage from Televisión Azteca. Journalist Manuel López San Martín even traveled by motorcycle to reach the area of the demonstration and interview them.

"We arrived at the FGR with the searching mothers who have been in a sit-in for 12 days If the authorities don't listen to them, we do Margarita López denounces harassment and intimidation instead of institutional support," the Azteca commentator posted this past September 21.

On her X account, Margarita López has expressed her gratitude to the Mayor of Cuauhtémoc, Alessandra Rojo de la Vega, for her support for the sit-in. "Thank you @AlessandraRdlv for all the support you have given us," she wrote.

PAN Members, Somos, Roberto Madrazo and Others Also Amplified It

Although no further evidence was presented in Óscar Balderas's column, opposition politicians replicated the version of the supposed discovery of "1,600 baby teeth" in a grave in Michoacán.

"We will not allow this news from Óscar Balderas to be forgotten. I will present a point of agreement in the @senadomexicano to demand an investigation into this clandestine grave. There cannot be a country at peace while parents search for their babies in the ashes. It is time to talk about the issues that really matter. Enough impunity!" said PAN Senator Laura Esquivel, showing a printed copy of Balderas's column, in addition to requesting a minute of silence in the Chamber for that case.

"THE HORROR. In Zacapu, Michoacán, searching mothers found 1,600 baby teeth in a clandestine grave. This means that hundreds of children between 1 and 6 years old were incinerated there… we are talking about hundreds of innocent children murdered," posted PAN Deputy América Rangel.

In the case of the Somos party, Emilio Álvarez Icaza reposted TV Azteca's interview with Óscar Balderas and wrote: "UNACCEPTABLE! While the government is busy pushing an anti-meme Law to control how it is criticized and keep its incompetence from being exposed, Mexico faces real problems that cannot be covered up with censorship."

Roberto Madrazo also spread one of Azteca's videos about the case, and stated on X: "THIS IS BRUTAL!!! It was not the prosecutor's office that found the charred remains of children. It was the searching mothers, because the government is more worried about there not being people with dual nationality, or about sanctioning memes and silencing media and social networks."
