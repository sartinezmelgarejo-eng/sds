This article by Mariana Álvarez Torres originally appeared in the September 27, 2026 edition of SinEmbargo, an independent Mexican digital news outlet.

Mexico City, September 27 (SinEmbargo).— President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo (CSP) concludes this Sunday a tour of the country's 32 states with an event in the Zócalo of Mexico City (CdMx), where she will report on the main actions carried out during the first two years of her Government.

At the event, scheduled for 11:00 on the esplanade of the capital's Zócalo, thousands of citizens and supporters are present, having come to hear the message of the Chief Executive following the activities she led over recent weeks.

During her message, Sheinbaum was greeted with shouts of "you are not alone." Before those gathered, the President affirmed that "we Mexicans do not want interference or interventionism" and maintained that no foreign power nor economic or political elite has the right to dictate the principle of the Homeland.

Live Updates

September 27, 2026, 11:18 a.m.

Sheinbaum Begins Her Message in the Zócalo of CdMx

Sheinbaum Pardo thanked the residents of Mexico City for coming to the Zócalo to accompany her at the close of her accountability tour. She also recognized the Head of Government, Clara Brugada, and her Cabinet for accompanying her during the first years of her Administration.

"We are closing a historic accountability tour, almost two years after the first woman President took office at the head of the Presidency of the Republic," she said.

11:09 a.m.

Brugada Calls for Backing Sheinbaum in the Face of the Shadows of Imperialist Ambition

The Head of Government of Mexico City, Clara Brugada, welcomed President Claudia Sheinbaum to the capital's Zócalo and highlighted actions of her Administration, among them the reduction of more than 50 percent in homicides, the increase in the minimum wage and the social programs.

Brugada also highlighted the mobility projects in the capital and affirmed that CdMx will become "the great railway gateway of the country." During her message, she called for maintaining popular unity, the unity of the transformation movement and national unity around Sheinbaum's leadership.

In the face of what she described as the "shadows of imperialist ambition," the capital's head of government asked for support for the Chief Executive and defended the country's sovereignty.

"Mexico is a free and sovereign country. Mexico is nobody's backyard. Mexico extends its hand, but never bows its head," she stressed.

11:08 a.m.

Honors to the Flag Begin in the Zócalo of CdMx

With the performance of the Mexican National Anthem, the honors to the Flag began in the Zócalo of Mexico City, prior to the message of President Claudia Sheinbaum.

10:59 a.m.

Sheinbaum Leaves the National Palace and Greets Citizens

Accompanied by her husband, Jesús María Tarriba Unger, President Claudia Sheinbaum left the National Palace for the podium installed in the Zócalo. Before approaching the public, she went to the color guard to render honors and salute the Flag.

10:08 a.m.

Rosa Icela Highlights the Arrival of Hundreds at the Zócalo to Hear Sheinbaum's Message

Post by @rosaicela_ on X

Through a post on X, formerly Twitter, the Secretary of Governance, Rosa Icela Rodríguez, highlighted that from very early on hundreds of people began to arrive at the Zócalo of Mexico City to hear the Chief Executive.

10:07 a.m.

There Are More Than 200,000 People in the Zócalo, CdMx Reports

Post by @craviotocesar on X

Minutes before the event began, Secretary César Cravioto Romero reported a turnout of 200,000 citizens on the esplanade of the capital's Zócalo.

Through a video posted on his X account, the official detailed that more people are expected to arrive, who will be able to watch the report on the screens installed in the surrounding streets.

9:21 a.m.

The Zócalo Looks Almost Full for Sheinbaum's Event

Post by @webcamsdemexico on X

More than an hour before the event began, the Plaza de la Constitución registers a large turnout of people gathering to hear the President's message to close her "Honesty and Results" tour. So far, around 70,000 citizens are reported in the vicinity.

7:27 a.m.

Cravioto Supervises Mobility and Access to the Zócalo for Sheinbaum's Rally

Post by @craviotocesar on X

The Secretary of Government of Mexico City (CdMx), César Cravioto Romero, indicated that since early this Sunday he has been supervising mobility and access to the event of President Claudia Sheinbaum's Second Government Report, from the Monitoring Center of the Traffic Undersecretariat.

"We are working to ensure orderly access and that everyone enjoys her message," the official wrote on X, formerly Twitter.
