This article by Alonso Urrutia and Alma E. Muñoz originally appeared in the October 14, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. In response to the mobilization of IMSS workers demanding a larger salary increase and denouncing shortages and working conditions, President Claudia Sheinbaum maintained that all legitimate demands will be addressed.

She announced that she instructed the director of IMSS, Zoé Robledo, to prepare a diagnosis of the shortages in order to correct them. At the same time, she asked the Secretariat of Health to prepare a diagnosis of the missing medicines in order to seek to supply them, but based on reports directly from the pharmacies in clinics and hospitals, not from the warehouses.

After showing videos that, she said, were taken from social media, to gauge the size of the turnout at the march, she questioned the positions of the Labor Party (PT) on the conflict, considering them opportunistic in electoral times.

Sheinbaum announced that after the diagnosis is gathered, the direct supply of medicines to clinics and hospitals will be addressed. She noted that for a month and a half a survey has been conducted with users of the pharmacies of IMSS, ISSSTE, and IMSS-Bienestar to identify which medicine shortages they have detected.

She said that the demand of the doctors who were hired during the covid-19 pandemic, and who today are demanding greater legal certainty about their employment situation, will be addressed so that they can receive permanent positions. She also confirmed that the pay of resident doctors, which is very low, will be increased, for which the possibilities for a raise are already being analyzed to determine the amount.

Sheinbaum mentioned that she instructed IMSS staff to guarantee that the consultation among workers on whether they accept the salary increase proposal be completely free and that there be no attempt to influence it.

She insisted that a great deal of work has been done in the health sector and that actions will be reinforced, but once again questioned the opportunism of the opposition parties and right-wing sectors with their load of messages and bots on social media.
