Mexico City, October 17 (SinEmbargo).— César Gustavo Jáuregui Moreno, Maru Campos's former Attorney General, was arrested on Saturday morning. He is the first victim claimed by the scandal over the intervention of two agents of the Central Intelligence Agency (CIA) in a state operation against a narco lab in the Sierra of Chihuahua last April.

The record published in the National Registry of Detentions (RND) of the Secretariat of Security and Citizen Protection (SSPC) detailed that the capture took place at 10:20 a.m. on La Cantera Avenue, at the George Washington Avenue bridge, in the Campestre Washington neighborhood, in the capital city of Chihuahua.

The record indicates that Jáuregui Moreno was apprehended by State Police officers and placed at the disposal of the State Attorney General's Office, Central-South Zone. At the time the record was consulted, at 11:37 a.m., the official was "in transit" to the Public Prosecutor's facilities.

Local media reported that the arrest was carried out after a Judge issued an arrest warrant related to criminal case 3055/2026, for the alleged crimes of unlawful exercise of public service and coalition.

According to judicial order 53095/2026, Judge Luis Alberto Simental Ortega yesterday authorized the arrest of Jáuregui Moreno and of Guillermo Arturo Zuany Portillo, former head of the State Investigation Agency (AEI).

In the latter's case, a charge of false statements before authorities with an aggravated penalty, provided for in Article 308 of the Penal Code of the State of Chihuahua, is also contemplated.

Jáuregui Defends Operation Days Before His Arrest

Last Thursday, October 15, two days before his capture, Jáuregui Moreno publicly defended his actions in the operation against the narco lab and said he was ready to assume any responsibility the authorities attributed to him.

"What would truly have been unforgivable would have been to be remiss and lukewarm in the fight against organized crime. If today, for those events, in which I am perfectly clear there is no crime whatsoever on my part, the Federation or the State holds me responsible, I am ready to assume it with pride," he stated in a video posted on his Instagram account.

His statements came after PAN Governor María Eugenia Campos confirmed that the State Attorney General's Office and the Attorney General's Office of the Republic (FGR) were conducting investigations into the intervention carried out in April. According to her statements, the officials carried out operations without keeping her informed of the decisions they made.
