This article by Ángeles Cruz Martínez originally appeared in the October 19, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Qinhuangdao. Behind the high speed, safety and stability of journeys across China’s train network there is a thriving industry dedicated to manufacturing inputs such as tracks and rail switching systems, which increasingly uses innovative technology, including robotics, to increase its efficiency.

The sector was born in this city, with which it has grown in parallel, since one of the first railway lines to reach Beijing was built here, and from this same site came the first steel bridges connecting towns and the metal structures necessary for the circulation of trains.

In turn, Qinhuangdao, in Hebei province, some 300 kilometers east of the country’s capital, became a commercial port starting in the late 19th century and, more recently, a tourist area of national importance.

Here lies the eastern end of the Great Wall of China, with the Dragon’s Head that juts into the Bohai Sea. It is also the only city whose name originates from an emperor, Qin Shihuang, the first in the Asian nation.

It also stands out as the seat of a technological development zone, where companies such as Citic Dicastal are located, the largest supplier of aluminum wheels and chassis parts for motor vehicles.

During a visit to the company by journalists from Latin America participating in the program of the China International Press Communication Center, Feng Liang, general manager of Foreign Operations, asserted that one in every three cars on the road in the world uses its products, including Formula One.

The company, focused on technological innovation, has more than 8,000 patents.

Citic Dicastal has a history that began in 1988 and just a decade later it was already in the international market through an agreement with the German automaker Audi. It arrived in Mexico in 2022 with two plants that produce around 3 million aluminum wheels a year, the executive commented.

Meanwhile, in the Shanhaiguan district is the company China Railway Shanqiao, identified as the “cradle” of steel components, indispensable for the operation of trains.

Besides being a pioneer in the sector, its growth has been constant. According to official information, it produces around 500,000 tons of bridges of said metal and 10,000 sets of railway turnouts, which switch trains from one track to another, per year.

The company was founded in 1894 and among its first activities was manufacturing the steel structures for the bridges that, in different areas of Chinese territory, cross the Yangtze River.

High Speed

It later joined the construction of the railway network that now measures 165,000 kilometers, of which almost a third are tracks for high-speed trains that reach 350 kilometers per hour (km/h).

China Railway Shanhaiguan Bridge Group –full name of China Railway Shanqiao– provides the inputs that make possible journeys in which high speed is only perceived through the windows, by the more than rapid passing of trees, light poles and towns where the train does not stop.

A sense of the speed at which the unit travels comes from the screen in each car that, among other data, reports the number of kilometers traveled per hour. Inside, passengers chat, read or sleep without any jolt.

Everything begins in the factory with the steel sections that later become track crossings, rails and sleepers, and where more than 3,000 people work to meet domestic and international demand.

The company’s products have reached countries such as Chile and Argentina in Latin America.

In Mexico it has collaborated with the firms in charge of building some sections of the Tren Maya, reported Damon Cai, general manager for the Americas region of China Railway Shanqiao, during a visit to the corporation’s facilities.

He said that in 2021 the company designed products with a cost of 10 million dollars for that project. In addition, the company is following the preparations for the renovation of Line 3 of the Metro in Mexico City. The company’s expectation is to send inputs with an approximate cost of 20 million dollars.

According to official data, it has reached the markets of Asia, the United States, Canada, Europe and Russia.

In addition to alliances with companies that have international reach, China Railway Shanqiao has made relevant technological progress. In 2024 it began producing pure steel with reduced impurities and an increase in the useful life of railway components.

In the factory there is an intelligent production area for high-quality handling of track switching systems, and robots specialized in making welding material, which never stop, while only one person watches at close range to make sure nothing fails.
