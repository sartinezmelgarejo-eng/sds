This article by Alonso Urrutia and Lilian Hernández originally appeared in the October 19, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. President Claudia Sheinbaum acknowledged that during telephone conversations with her US counterpart, Donald Trump, he has proposed that Mexico increase its imports of US natural gas. In response, she commented, she has told him that future projections for our country include increasing the extraction of that fuel in Mexico.

During her press conference, the president noted that in the talks she has had with the magnate, he has also asked whether US companies could help in Mexico in oil matters. She said that in both cases she has clearly told him that there are constitutional restrictions on investment in oil, but she has also told him that there are US companies that participate with Pemex and could expand their investment through that route.

Asked directly, she acknowledged that in some bilateral talks, Trump has told her directly that there are territories in Mexico controlled by organized crime, to which she has rejected this interpretation, arguing that if this were true, fentanyl trafficking would not have fallen by 70 percent nor homicides in Mexico by fifty percent.

She specified that when they have addressed the issue, Trump has never used the term narco-politicians, and in all cases the security issue has been addressed not in confrontational terms but rather in a friendly manner. Sheinbaum denied that at any point in the telephone conversations Trump has sought to condition the signing of the trade agreement in exchange for security measures, since collaboration in these terms has been channeled through another sphere.

On another note, she highlighted the lifting of the sanctions that US authorities had maintained against Mexican air transport. She said that at the time, even that country’s cargo airlines refused to move their operations from the Felipe Ángeles International Airport back to the Mexico City International Airport.
