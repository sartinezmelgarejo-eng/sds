This article by Fernando Camacho and Enrique Méndez originally appeared in the October 20, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. The holding of a religious event on Monday in the Chamber of Deputies, organized by PRI deputy Israel Betanzos and the association Policías Celestiales (Heavenly Police), which was attended by a representative of the Israeli embassy, drew rejection from Morena deputies, who considered that it violated the secular nature of the Mexican State.

In the final part of Tuesday's plenary session, from her seat, legislator Magdalena Rosales Cruz requested a minute of silence and reproached that “the situation in Palestine marks three years of intensification of the conflict since October 2023, a period that various international organizations have formally described as genocide, with a deliberate focus on attacking children and destroying the Palestinian population.”

In that context, she emphasized, “yesterday, in this secular chamber of the Mexican State, the evangelical Christian religious association Policías Celestiales, which lives by the motto ‘Armed forces at the service of God and society,’ was received and honored, accompanied by the Israeli embassy.”

The legislator warned that “the relationship between Israel and evangelical groups, particularly those aligned with so-called Christian Zionism, is a decisive strategic and geopolitical alliance. The growth of evangelical communities in the Latin American region has reshaped local politics.”

In this regard, she added that “the government of Israel has actively implemented ‘faith diplomacy,’ tightening diplomatic ties with Latin American pastors, religious leaders, and legislators to secure votes and support in international forums. It is a symbiotic pact in which Israel obtains an unconditional political and financial shield in the West.”

She also noted that Leonel Guillermoprieto is “known as Comandante Chihuahua, founder of the Policía Celestial association, arrested in 2007 in the United States for drug trafficking. They carry out so-called ‘heavenly operations’ through motivational talks with a religious background in various police stations around the country.”

She also recalled that “the first records of activity begin in 2015, through the National Congress of Police at the Service of God and Society. In 2017 they signed their first collaboration agreement with the Special Commission against Human Trafficking of the Chamber of Deputies and its chair, PRI deputy Julieta Fernández Márquez, from the state of Guerrero, to give these religious talks in 10 states of the Republic.”

Fernández Márquez is the wife of the coordinator of the PRI senators, Manuel Añorve.

Rosales emphasized that “there have been many martyrs to achieve the separation of church and state. Therefore, this secular State, this Chamber of Deputies, refuses to accept the continued presence of religious groups in this chamber, which is also a tribute to the heroes of the homeland.”

Immediately, her fellow party member Beatriz Navarro joined in to “expose the unprecedented event” convened on Monday by PRI deputy Israel Betanzos, when, she cited, Article 40 of the Constitution establishes that Mexico is a secular State.

Navarro complained that the organization of police officers, both retired and active, had given awards to opposition mayors “and to the Israeli embassy, who are genocidal, since they have killed more than 74,000 Palestinians and wounded more than 175,000 residents of Gaza in less than three years. This only reflects their inhumanity and what they are: genocidal.”

Giving them an award, she added, “is an offense to the memory of the victims and to the dignity that this Chamber should represent. No one denies them their right to meet and to think as they wish, but do it somewhere else. This space is not yours: it belongs to the people of Mexico and it is for the defense of human rights.”

PAZ Operative at the Meeting

Separately, in an interview with this newspaper, the deputy coordinator of the Morena caucus, Dolores Padierna Luna, alluded, without mentioning her name, to Morena deputy Carolina Anda González, who was also present at Monday's award ceremony, and considered that she acted against the support her party has expressed for Palestine and its people.

“This Morena deputy who was there is the alternate of Hugo Éric (Flores, current leader of the PAZ). They operate with religious matters and don't quite understand that this doesn't go with politics. We all have freedom of worship, but we cannot violate the Constitution in this Chamber,” she said.

Anda González is identified as a financial operative of the PAZ, the third party created by Hugo Éric Flores, and is also one of the people closest to the Morelos lawyer.

Padierna said there are deputies who do not want to accept Morena's political line, “who put on the cap of citizens or unions or civil society when Morena's political line doesn't suit them. And they are Morena members for electoral purposes and for other things.

“So I would say that Morena, the Honesty and Justice Commission, has to enforce the political line set by our national councils. We don't act on our own; we have a political line to respect, and it must be respected by everyone.”

Secular State, Not Jacobin: PRI

In the morning, the coordinator of the PRI deputies, Rubén Moreira, defended Monday's event, backed his fellow party member Israel Betanzos, and denied that it had been an affront to the secular State.

“I did not see a liturgical act, I saw a ceremony in which some awards were given, and of course (Betanzos) has the support of our caucus. The Chamber must be open to all kinds of things,” he cut in during an interview.

—Isn't the secular nature of the State affected?

—Secular is one thing and Jacobin is another. The secular State is not affected by that; it is affected when decisions are made that are removed from science, from democracy. When public decisions are made like those (former President Andrés Manuel) López Obrador made, those of the “detente” (in reference to a religious image, during the Covid pandemic). That is where there is a problem with the secular State,” he argued.

—Doesn't it give a bad image because of the genocide against the Palestinian people? he was pressed.

—This is a place where ambassadors from all countries are welcome, and I don't think it is a place for us to question that. We must remember that the conduct of the Mexican State in international matters corresponds to the head of the Mexican State.

When asked why the PRI invited Leonel Guillermoprieto to the chamber, despite his criminal record in Mexico and the United States, he replied: “I don't know him.”
