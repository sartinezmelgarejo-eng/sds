This article by Sugeyry Romina Gándara originally appeared in the October 23, 2026 edition of SinEmbargo, an independent Mexican digital news outlet.

The Governor of Chihuahua blamed the former prosecutor for allowing the entry of US agents to operate illegally on national territory.

Mexico City, October 23 (SinEmbargo).– Governor María Eugenia "Maru" Campos Galván accused her former prosecutor, César Gustavo Jáuregui Moreno, of being the one who allowed CIA agents to enter Chihuahua and accused him of being corrupt. She did so during the difficult meeting she had with the President of Mexico on September 19 of this year. Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo recommended that she go to the Attorney General's Office of the Republic (FGR), and she did so. The Head of the Mexican State said this morning, at her press conference, that the Chihuahua governor went before Ernestina Godoy and there ratified her statements against her fellow party member and political running mate of many years.

"When I told her 'agents came in,' she told me 'it wasn't me, it was my former prosecutor.' But she didn't only tell me that, she told me that they had found corruption that the former prosecutor had committed," the President recounted.

Campos says César Jáuregui owns gambling casinos.

"She told me that the former prosecutor has casinos, that this is what they had found when she moved the Prosecutor. What did the President tell her? 'Proceed, as any citizen should proceed when they find acts that violate the Law,'" Sheinbaum added.

In light of the complaint made by the Governor of Chihuahua against her former prosecutor, President Sheinbaum recommended that the state Prosecutor's Office begin the corresponding procedure to investigate this case and offered to put her in contact with the head of the FGR to report these acts.

"I told her, 'why doesn't the Prosecutor's Office of Justice of the State of Chihuahua proceed, and if you like I'll mention it to the Attorney General of the Republic so she can receive you,'" the Head of the Executive recounted.

Sheinbaum revealed that Maru Campos did meet with Ernestina Godoy, before whom she ratified her complaint against Jáuregui.

"The Attorney General can tell what the Governor of Chihuahua told her, since from what the Attorney General tells me, she told her exactly the same thing: that the person responsible for agents having entered Chihuahua is the former prosecutor and that they had also found acts of corruption," she said.

The President and the Governor Exchanged Reproaches During Their Meeting

President Claudia Sheinbaum confirmed what Governor Maru Campos said during her interview with Latinus: that during their meeting, held this past September 19, both exchanged reproaches and even raised their voices.

According to Sheinbaum's account, Maru Campos reproached her because, according to the Governor, she had accused her of having committed Treason, while the President expressed her annoyance because the Chihuahua governor described her Administration as "the Government of death."

"She reproached me for why I had accused her of Treason. I told her 'no, it has to be investigated why foreign agents entered without permission and in accordance with the National Security Law," the Head of the Executive detailed.

"I told her 'I didn't accuse you, I said that the Government of Chihuahua or someone had violated the National Security Law. And you said that we were the Government of death, well, what is that?', I told her," she added.

President Sheinbaum added that at the end of her meeting with Maru Campos she told her that the relationship between them would be maintained solely through institutional channels, and so she reiterated that the federal Government would provide support to the Government of Chihuahua in everything it needs.

"When the conversation ends she told me 'well, how will the relationship continue?' and I told her 'institutionally. If you have a security issue you can talk to the Secretary of Security. If you have an issue that has to do with any other institutional matter, between the federal Government and the Government of Chihuahua, with the Secretariat of Governance we will help you with everything you need," Sheinbaum recounted.

Maru Campos Reveals Details of Her Meeting With Sheinbaum

The Governor of Chihuahua, Maru Campos, revealed that her meeting with President Sheinbaum, which took place this past September 19 during her visit to the state, was a tense meeting in which they exchanged reproaches and even raised their voices, this in an interview with Latinus.

The state governor recounted that her meeting with the Head of the Executive took place in "a gloomy, sad office, with no supplies on the desks, sort of abandoned, dirty. They sit me in an office at a tiny desk [...] with some blank executive cards, some pencils, and I sit down to wait for her."

Campos recounted that, after a wait of approximately five minutes, the President arrived at the office and "there was a very strong, very tense conversation, a conversation full of reproaches, of mutual complaints. They were complaints, mutual reproaches above all about the security situation in the state of Chihuahua and about the security situation in the country."

"She was very angry and I was also very angry. We raised our voices, both of us. We both raised our voices because she had a grievance over my having brought the American agents into Mexico, supposedly. But my grievance was because 19 consecutive times from the highest tribune in this country, at the mañanera, I was accused of Treason," she noted.

Maru Campos admitted that her meeting with President Sheinbaum did not end well and added that from the start of the meeting the situation was tense, since "she went straight to unloading that anger, to unloading those emotions she had not been able to unload, that was September 19, more than a month ago, and I had not seen her since April 19."

According to the Governor's statement, the Head of the Executive reproached her for "bringing in the Americans through the back door. You generated a conflict with the United States and you are the only Governor with whom I cannot have a good relationship."

According to Maru Campos, the three reasons the President reproached her were "the American agents at the narco-lab, the grievance because you called me 'the President of death' [...] and a third, that there were American agents inside the Torre Centinela."
