This article by La Jornada Editorial Staff originally appeared in the October 24, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. Ahead of the mobilizations announced for next Monday, the Secretariat of Governance (SG) reiterated its call for dialogue with the transport organizations, to address their proposals through institutional channels.

“From the first moment, Segob formally offered a space for dialogue and a permanent working table with the competent authorities, in order to listen to the sector's demands and make progress in building agreements,” it stated.

Governance acknowledged the concerns expressed by the organizations, and said that understanding and collaboration make it possible to find solutions to their proposals.

“Within this framework, it calls for prioritizing dialogue and avoiding actions that affect the citizenry, traffic and the transport of goods,” it said.

It noted that it will remain open to communication with the sector to address its demands and favor agreements through institutional channels.
