This article by La Jornada Editorial Staff originally appeared in the October 25, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. The National Chamber of Freight Transport (Canacar) announced on Sunday, through a post on X, a national strike for October 26 in protest for better security conditions on the highways.

“Tomorrow, October 26, we truckers will carry out a national strike to make visible what we move every day: the food, the medicines and the goods that sustain the country's economy. We demand to be heard and attended to with concrete solutions that improve the conditions of the people who move Mexico. Transporting cannot continue to be synonymous with risking one's life and assets,” the statement reads.

For its part, the Secretariat of Governance (SG) stated that the majority of the trucking organizations agreed to sit down to dialogue to address their proposals and avoid the mobilization, and it also expressed surprise at the chamber's decision to call its members to strike, stating in a communiqué:

The SG acknowledged the associations and chambers that are members of Canacar that distanced themselves from the mobilization, stating that most of them publicly expressed their conviction not to affect the population.

Finally, the secretariat invited the chamber's leadership to join the dialogue, with a table that remains open, asserting that the institutional route is the way to address the sector's proposals.
