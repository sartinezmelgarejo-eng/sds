This article by Ángel Bolaños Sánchez originally appeared in the October 25, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City is the entity with the largest public investment in the country this year, with 57,911 million pesos, 14.4 percent of the national total.

The annual report that the Secretary of Administration and Finance, Juan Pablo de Botton, sent to the local Congress ahead of his appearance on Tuesday highlights the boost to investment brought by the Infrastructure, Mobility, Water and Security Trust, created in 2025 to capture the resources from the increase in the payroll tax from 3 to 4 percent –with an estimated 10 billion pesos a year– and the reconfiguration of the Mixed Fund for Tourism Promotion, which made it possible to secure more than 23 billion pesos for some 2,000 public works to host the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

The document highlights that the city is also the entity with the highest tax collection in the country, with more own-source revenue than federal revenue, and reports a preliminary amount at the close of the third quarter of the year of 265,848.8 million pesos, 5.4 percent more than in the same period of 2025.

The report describes the cadastre modernization program, which has no tax implications, to keep the information on the city's properties up to date with physical, legal and economic data, such as location, size, construction characteristics, ownership, liens and value, reflecting changes in ownership, new construction and modifications, and citizen collaboration is important.

The digitalization aims to make it easy for citizens to consult information and carry out procedures on their properties quickly and simply: “its purpose is to provide information and legal certainty to protect the right to private property and to have accurate data that dispel any concern over property assets.”

In addition, it highlights the consolidation of the Permanent Driver's License Program, which combines the reduction of recurring procedures with in-person and digital service mechanisms, and which has had high public acceptance, issuing 2,245,000 license cards between November 16, 2024 and this past September 30.
