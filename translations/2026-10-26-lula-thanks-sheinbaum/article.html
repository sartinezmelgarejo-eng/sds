Mexico City, October 26 (SinEmbargo).– The recently reelected President of Brazil, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, thanked the President of Mexico, Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo, for the message of support and congratulations she conveyed to him this Monday in a phone call for the victory in yesterday's election.

"Thank you very much, dear President Claudia Sheinbaum. It was a pleasure to receive your phone call today. This is not only a victory of the Brazilian people, but of all Latin American democracies," the president said on his official X account.

The president emphasized that his victory will allow him to keep fostering cooperation and bringing closer both nations governed by the left, stating:

Post by @LulaOficial on X

When the People Decide to Defend Their Rights They Win: CSP

President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo said this Monday morning that neither interference nor social media will triumph over the organized people, as Brazil showed in the runoff of the presidential elections held on Sunday, in which Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva achieved a historic comeback despite the interventionism of the United States (US), Meta and X to favor Flávio Bolsonaro.

The federal president vindicated the democratic triumph of the Brazilian nation and maintained that when peoples mobilize in defense of their rights, they win.

-What lessons does this election in Brazil leave for the left in Latin America and for Mexico, where we do not have a runoff?, she was asked at the morning press conference.

-Always get close to the people. Have as a vision, as an objective, to deliver for the people and allow the people to decide. And the people of Brazil gave the world a lesson that beyond the use of social media -which, by the way, in the middle of election day, they denounced that one of the platforms, Meta, was benefiting Bolsonaro over Lula-, and however much social media, well, the one who triumphs is the people. That is very good, very good, because what it shows is that when the people decide to mobilize, to defend their rights, they triumph, always. And that is a lesson for Brazil, for Mexico and for the entire world- Sheinbaum Pardo asserted.

World Leaders Congratulate Lula

Leaders from Latin America, Europe and Asia congratulated President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva for his triumph in the runoff, with which he secured yesterday a fourth non-consecutive term after defeating Senator Flávio Bolsonaro, the far-right opposition candidate.

Post by @DiazCanelB on X

While thousands of supporters took to the streets of Brazil to celebrate the continuity of the leader of the Workers' Party (PT), who called for maintaining the unity and mobilization of his followers, messages of recognition arrived from different points of the world. In them, presidents and representatives of different political currents recognized the electoral result and expressed their interest in maintaining or strengthening diplomatic and cooperation ties with the South American country.

In a message addressed to the Brazilian president, the President of Cuba, Miguel Díaz-Canel, described the result as a “historic victory” that will allow the path of peace and social justice in Brazil to continue.

"I congratulate you on behalf of the Cuban government and people for this historic victory that guarantees the continuity of the path of peace, sovereignty and social justice undertaken by the Brazilian people under your leadership," he wrote on X, formerly Twitter.

Post by @ABDELAESPRIELLA on X

The President of Colombia, Abelardo de la Espriella, recognized the victory of Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva and stated that, despite their political and ideological differences, both countries must maintain a relationship based on mutual respect and cooperation.

“Neither does Lula decide for Colombians nor do I decide for Brazilians. The people decide! And when the people speak at the ballot box, their will is respected because it is sacred. That is what democracy is about!” he wrote on social media.

For his part, the Panamanian president, José Raúl Mulino, also expressed his willingness to continue cooperation between both nations.

“I congratulate the Brazilian people for this democratic day and especially President Lula da Silva for the victory. We will continue working together for our countries!” he posted on X.

Post by @JoseRaulMulino on X

Beijing Highlights Shared Future With Brazil

On the occasion of the congratulations, the spokesperson for the Chinese Foreign Ministry, Guo Jiakun, stated this Monday that his country is prepared to work with the Brazilian government in building a community with a shared future.

“China congratulates Lula on his victory in the presidential runoff and is ready to work with Brazil to continue advancing in building a China-Brazil community with a shared future,” he said at a press conference.

Putin Congratulates Lula on His Victory and Upcoming Birthday

The President of Russia, Vladimir Putin, congratulated Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva on his electoral victory in Brazil and stated that the results reflect citizen support for his agenda of economic and social development, as well as the defense of the country's sovereignty.

In a letter addressed to the Brazilian president, Putin called him “dear friend” and highlighted his leadership qualities, in addition to highlighting his contribution to strengthening the strategic partnership between both nations.

The Russian president expressed his intention to maintain bilateral cooperation in different areas, as well as to promote joint work in multilateral organizations such as the United Nations (UN), BRICS and the G20.

Iran Wishes Lula Success in His New Term

The President of Iran, Masoud Pezeshkian, congratulated Lula da Silva on his victory in the runoff of the presidential elections and expressed his interest in expanding relations between both countries during the new term.

"I hope that during your new term, the friendly relations between Iran and Brazil will expand even further and that cooperation between both countries in areas of common interest will advance, in line with the common interests of the two nations and the strengthening of multilateralism, development and international peace," he stated in the message released by the Iranian press, cited by Europa Press.
