This article by La Jornada Editorial Staff originally appeared in the October 27, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

President Claudia Sheinbaum reported that her phone conversation with her Brazilian counterpart, Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, was brief but emotional because he was very happy about his victory in the second round of the presidential elections last Sunday. She shared that the talk was very brief, during which he invited her to visit Brazil and, in turn, she also extended an invitation for the Brazilian president to visit our country soon.
