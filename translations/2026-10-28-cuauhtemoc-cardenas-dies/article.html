This article by La Jornada Editorial Staff originally appeared in the October 28, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

The death of Engineer Cuauhtémoc Cárdenas, historic leader of the Mexican left, has been reported at the age of 92.

Cuauhtémoc Cárdenas left the PRI after considering that the party had strayed from the principles that gave rise to it in the Mexican Revolution and that the imposition by the President of the Republic in the naming of who would be the presidential candidate should not be accepted.

For more than a year he sought supporters for the democratic cause, and on October 14, 1987, the leader of the Authentic Party of the Mexican Revolution, Carlos Cantú Rosas, nominated Cuauhtémoc Cárdenas as its candidate for the Presidency of the Republic. Later the Popular Socialist Party and the Cardenist Front for National Reconstruction Party joined, and they formed the National Democratic Front (FDN), with other small groups such as the Social Democratic Party, the Mexican Green Party and later the Movement for Socialism, which was made up of splinters from the Unified Socialist Party of Mexico, the Mexican Workers' Party and the Revolutionary Workers' Party.

It was then that he launched himself as presidential candidate of the so-called National Democratic Front in 1988, while the PRI nominated Carlos Salinas de Gortari. On July 6, the day of the presidential elections, in the face of the famous crash of the system, Cárdenas, Manuel J. Clouthier (PAN candidate) and Rosario Ibarra de Piedra (of the PRT) set aside their differences and went to protest before the Secretariat of Governance. The trends favored Cárdenas, who declared himself the winner with a trend of 42 percent, to 36 for Salinas, with 50 percent of polling stations counted. Then the protests over the alleged electoral fraud began, the mass rallies were repeated, and many were waiting for Cárdenas's call to defend the vote at the ballot boxes, but it did not happen because, according to his collaborators, a civil war could have been unleashed.

Founder of the PRD

After the 1988 election he chose to found a political party to carry forward the goals of his campaign. Thus the PRD was born in 1989. He was again a presidential candidate in 1994 and 2000, and in 1997 he became the first elected head of government of the capital.
