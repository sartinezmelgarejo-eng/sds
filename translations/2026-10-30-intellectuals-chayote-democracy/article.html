This article by Alma E. Muñoz originally appeared in the October 30, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. The right-wing intellectuals who attack us "long for the democracy in which they received checks; it is the democracy of the chayote that they defend, the one of the coddling," and deep down they are "racist and classist, they are bothered that the people rule," said President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo, who questioned Ernesto Zedillo over his publication "We Live in an Electoral Autocracy." How dare he!, she said, when he negotiated with the United States "the arrival of Vicente Fox to the Presidency of the Republic against the popular will."

In addition, "he was part of the electoral frauds, particularly that of '88, because although he later falls out with (Carlos) Salinas de Gortari, he was part of his cabinet."

In her press conference, she said that intellectuals like Enrique Krauze and Héctor Aguilar Camín long for "their closeness to power, the dinners they attended with the President of the Republic, the private meetings, the advice they gave, the money they received, their co-optation."

She added: "in reality, that is what moves the right today" because "the intellectuals who now get together with (Ricardo) Salinas Pliego" are right-wing, but the problem is that in Mexico "the right as such has never had a place, because even when the PAN wins with Fox, with Calderón, they seek a more democratic discourse."

She recalled that those intellectuals "betrayed" Salinas de Gortari to go with Zedillo, then with Fox, Felipe Calderón and finally with Enrique Peña Nieto "because they all coddled them," and now "they are against us, well, each one in their place, because we don't buy anyone, we don't buy people's support. We are building a democratic country. For them, democracy is where they participated, where they were the owners and hierarchs of the word. No, now it is the people," she stated.

She commented that yesterday, "in the hatred they have against (Andrés Manuel) López Obrador, they were already saying that the president never recognized Cuauhtémoc Cárdenas. Do you know what López Obrador did when he won the election in 2018? The first person he went to visit the next day was Cárdenas Solórzano, to invite him to join the government." "They had their differences, yes, but it speaks to the greatness of a man," the recognition that the former federal executive gave to "the one who invited me to this movement of transformation and who made the first rupture of a corrupt and authoritarian regime."

They Abandoned Cuauhtémoc Cárdenas

The President maintained that some of the intellectuals who today criticize the 4T movement "had supported Ingeniero Cuauhtémoc Cárdenas, (Woldenberg, Aguilar Camín and all of them) they abandon him and say that, in 1988, it was not proven that there was fraud, and a little while later, hand in hand with Salinas de Gortari."

Today they talk about democracy "when they betrayed a citizens' movement that had fought to transform Mexico for years, but that was consolidated with social movements, political parties that came from the left, with this rupture of the PRI" that the Ingeniero led, she concluded.
