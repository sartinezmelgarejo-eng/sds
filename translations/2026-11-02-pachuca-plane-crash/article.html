Mexico City, November 2 (SinEmbargo).— A small plane crashed this Monday near Venta Prieta, south of the municipality of Pachuca, Hidalgo, which reportedly left two people dead, according to preliminary reports.

“The Government Secretariat is maintaining coordination with the authorities to follow up on the response to the incident. The population is asked to avoid the area, make way for emergency services and follow the instructions of the personnel working at the site,” the state agency said in a statement.

The accident occurred at around 9:00 a.m. near the Sam’s Club store located in the area, and just a few meters from the 18th Military Zone and the Juan Guillermo Villasana airport.

The crashed aircraft is a Cessna-type small plane with registration XB-AZG. After the impact, the aircraft caught fire, producing a thick column of smoke that could be seen from several points in the state capital.

The accident prompted the immediate mobilization of military personnel, who began the first security-perimeter tasks to facilitate the work of emergency services and members of the Fire Department.

Small Plane Crashes After Losing Altitude

Early reports and witnesses interviewed by local media who saw the incident said the plane appeared to have lost altitude before crashing into the commercial area.

After the impact, flames began to consume the aircraft, so the fire department worked for several minutes to put out the fire and prevent it from spreading to other areas of the shopping center.

Civil Protection (PC) personnel and Hidalgo’s Public Security Secretariat (SSP) went to the scene to help evacuate passersby and treat people possibly affected by the smoke.
