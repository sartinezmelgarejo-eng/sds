Mexico City, November 1 (SinEmbargo).— A storage unit that Thomas Markle, father of Meghan Markle, rented in Rosarito, Baja California, was reportedly used to store photographs, magazines and videos with alleged child pornography, according to an investigation by the British newspaper Daily Mail, whose journalists notified the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) of the United States (US) about the discovery.

According to the investigation, the property contained publications related to pedophilia, images of minors who were allegedly victims of sexual exploitation, 35-millimeter photographic negatives, seven hard drives and more than 50 videotapes. Among the belongings, documents with Internet addresses and references to the so-called dark web were also identified.

The discovery adds to the investigations that authorities in the Philippines are carrying out against Markle, 82, after his son, Thomas Markle Jr., reported him for alleged possession of child sexual abuse material and handed over four boxes of possible evidence to the police.

How Was the Content of Thomas Markle’s Storage Unit Discovered?

According to the British outlet, Thomas Markle rented the storage space in 2017, when he was living in Rosarito. However, he stopped making the monthly payments for about two years, which allowed the journalists to access the unit, 4.6 meters long by three meters wide, to examine the belongings that remained inside.

Among the documented items was a desktop computer, along with instructions for using a program designed to erase information from storage drives. The reporters also found various publications on sexuality and audiovisual material labeled with titles whose content they could not verify.

The investigation included an image in which the father of the Duchess of Sussex allegedly appears sitting next to a young woman on the bed of a hotel room. However, the newspaper acknowledged that it was unable to independently confirm the identity or age of the person photographed, and therefore could not determine the circumstances in which it was taken.

Mexican Authorities Secure Storage Unit in Rosarito; Markle Denies Accusations

For their part, members of the National Guard (GN) and personnel from the Baja California State Prosecutor’s Office carried out an operation on Saturday, October 31, at the property linked to Markle, after the possible existence of child sexual abuse material became public. The intervention was carried out under a court order that allowed the site to be inspected and items to be secured for analysis, the Daily Mail reported.

According to the newspaper, the warrant was requested by state prosecutors from Judge Estefany Moreno Gutiérrez, for the purpose of searching for evidence related to possible crimes of child sexual exploitation and human trafficking.

In the midst of these proceedings, the suspect rejected any link to the reported materials and even denied having rented a storage space in the border city.

“I didn’t have any storage unit in Rosarito!” he told the Daily Mail. He had previously also called his son’s accusations “sick lies” and asserted that he never had sexual relations with minors.

So far, Mexican authorities have not publicly reported the results of the forensic analyses, nor have any criminal charges against Markle been announced in the country. The seizure is an investigative measure and does not, by itself, establish his responsibility for the reported acts.

Philippines Orders Analysis of Thomas Markle’s Devices Over His Son’s Complaint

The investigations against Thomas Markle in the Philippines advanced on October 29, when a judge in Cebu City authorized the police to examine the electronic devices recovered from his former residence. The decision will make it possible to learn the content of the equipment, whose analysis had been pending for lack of a court order.

The proceeding arose from a complaint by Thomas Markle Jr., 60, who handed over four boxes of possible evidence to the authorities. According to Sky News, as cited by The Times, he warned that his father’s hard drives could contain “hundreds, if not thousands” of photographs and videos related to the sexual exploitation of minors.

The son of the former film lighting technician explained that his suspicions began when he went through a phone that had belonged to his father, where he said he found a video of alleged child sexual abuse. He also said he saw a photograph in which Markle supposedly appeared in a bed next to a girl who seemed to be underage.

The court authorization is a step toward determining whether the reported files exist and contain illicit material. For now, the accusations remain under investigation, and no ruling establishing the criminal responsibility of Meghan Markle’s father has been reported.

Meghan Markle Breaks Her Silence on Accusations Against Her Father

Meghan Markle, Duchess of Sussex, expressed her concern over the accusations against her father in a statement released on October 23, in which she explained that her estrangement from her family was tied to the need to protect her children, Archie and Lilibet, and not only to the personal differences that had been reported in the press.

“I am deeply disturbed by the news that has come to light in the last 24 hours, and my thoughts are with all the children who may have been harmed,” said Prince Harry’s wife, who also revealed that years ago she received information about accusations against her father.

The Duchess said British authorities had informed her of the existence of troubling allegations and that she understood they were under investigation.

London’s Metropolitan Police later confirmed that it received a letter in January 2019 and shared it with the Duke and Duchess of Sussex, although it did not pass the accusations on to other law enforcement agencies. Facing criticism over that omission, the institution announced an internal review and decided to refer the information to the relevant foreign authorities.
