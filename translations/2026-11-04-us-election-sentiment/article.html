This article by Anahí Del Ángel originally appeared in the November 4, 2026 edition of Revista Contralínea, a Mexican investigative journalism magazine.

The result of the midterm elections in the United States, in which the Democratic Party won a majority in the House of Representatives and in the Senate, shows the “sentiment of the American people,” said President Claudia Sheinbaum. In her morning press conference, she highlighted the victory of the governor of California, Xavier Becerra, and the governor-elect of Texas, Gina Hinojosa, both of Mexican descent.

The president recognized the participation of Mexican Americans in the election. As is known, yesterday 435 seats in the House of Representatives were up for election, of which the Democratic Party won 228, compared to 139 for the Republican Party; and 35 seats in the Senate, of which the Democrats won 20, while the Republicans only reached 13.

In addition, more than 30 governors were elected, among which Democratic victories stand out in states that had historically been governed by Republicans, such as Texas, with the election of Gina Hinojosa as governor, and the victory of James Talarico, the future senator.

When asked whether the election result could speed up the review of the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA), Sheinbaum Pardo explained that this issue is addressed with her US counterpart at the federal level. “That, let’s say, was not up for a vote: it was Congress and some of the states; so, the relationship with the federal government will depend on President Trump’s government itself and whether this election changes its point of view.”

The head of the federal Executive avoided anticipating the US government’s position regarding the Agreement, and said the negotiations continue to seek the best relationship with respect for sovereignty.
