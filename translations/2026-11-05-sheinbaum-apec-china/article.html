This article by Lilian Hernández and Alma E. Muñoz originally appeared in the November 5, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. The president of Mexico, Claudia Sheinbaum, confirmed that on November 15 she will travel to China to participate in the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Forum (APEC) Summit, which begins on the 17th of this month.

In her absence, she reported, the Secretary of Governance, Rosa Icela Rodríguez, will lead the morning press conference at the National Palace, since she will be out of the country from the 15th to the 20th.

She detailed that she will leave Mexico in the early morning of Sunday, November 15, and plans to return on Thursday the 19th, arriving on national territory in the early morning of November 20 to lead the ceremony for the Day of the Mexican Revolution.

During the press conference at the National Palace, she noted that her attendance also responds to the fact that Mexico will host APEC in 2028, so it is tradition that the leader of the country that will host the next edition participates in the previous meeting.

She confirmed a bilateral meeting with the president of China, Xi Jinping, and noted her interest in holding a meeting with the president of the United States, Donald Trump, although she clarified that the attendance of and meeting with the U.S. leader are not yet confirmed.
