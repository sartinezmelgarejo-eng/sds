This article by Alma E. Muñoz and Lilian Hernández originally appeared in the November 5, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. President Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo considered that the racism and the raids against migrants carried out by the administration of President Donald Trump contributed to Latinos voting for the Democratic Party last Tuesday.

“Yes, it is very likely,” she stated during her press conference, when asked about the subject.

She indicated that “recently analyses have been coming out of who voted for which political party (in the United States), and a lot of the Latino vote, among them those of Mexican origin, who had voted for Trump in 2024, no longer voted for the Republican Party, they voted for the Democratic Party.”
