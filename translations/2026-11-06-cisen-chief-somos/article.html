Mexico City, November 6 (SinEmbargo).— Juan Carlos Padilla Aguilar, former head of the Center for Research and National Security (CISEN), affirmed that he has not had contact for 30 years with Alfredo Hernández Casillas, linked to the network of electoral suppliers that is under investigation for irregularities in contracts with the National Electoral Institute (INE), and said that the mention made by President Claudia Sheinbaum about his relationship with that person is only meant to hit the Somos party.

In a statement, Padilla Aguilar maintained that he met Hernández Casillas in 1980 when he was appointed Director General of the National Population Registry, and that he worked with him for "a little more than 10 years."

"When the state government concluded in September 1993, our working relationship ended. For more than 30 years I have not had any kind of contact with that person," he stated.
