This article by Lilian Hernández and Emir Olivares originally appeared in the November 9, 2026 edition of La Jornada, Mexico's premier left wing daily newspaper.

Mexico City. To compensate for the damage caused by the toxic spill in the Sonora River in 2014, the president of Mexico, Claudia Sheinbaum, stated that Grupo México was forced to allocate 1.5 billion pesos, although she did not specify whether it has already been delivered or whether any amount is still pending.

When asked about this issue at the morning press conference, the president indicated that the company was forced to provide 1.5 billion to compensate for the damage and that the government would help the workers of Cananea.

She clarified that the agreement that was signed was to make the remediation effective, and the government contributed in order to "do justice to the workers."
