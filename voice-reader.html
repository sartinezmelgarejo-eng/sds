Voice Reader

Reads the article using your device's own voices — including Premium/Enhanced ones the terminal can't reach. Works best in Safari on your Mac.

Want the most realistic voices? System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → System Voice → Manage Voices → download e.g. Ava (Premium) or Evan (Enhanced). They'll appear in the list above after downloading. Voices marked (local) run offline; others may need internet.
